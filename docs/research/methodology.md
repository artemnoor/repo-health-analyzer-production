# Methodology archive

The backend preserves the validated methodology rather than the exploratory
repository snapshots. Collection produces normalized facts; each category
applies its calibrated policy; the frozen Score v1 engine combines only usable
numeric categories and carries evidence, coverage, confidence and limitations.

The production source-of-truth checks are the golden tests under
`tests/golden/repo_health/`. Historical research runs, copied repositories and
generated reports are intentionally not runtime inputs and are not shipped.
